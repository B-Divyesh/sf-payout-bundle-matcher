How your data stays private

Settlement Match processes customer and financial CSV data in your browser.

Data stored on this device

Imported rows, mappings, selections, reviewer notes, and signed reports are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB database.

A Pro license token and reusable column mapping are stored in localStorage. We do not receive your workspace data.

Network requests

Sociobot serves the application files. Restoring a Pro license sends that token only to the Sociobot billing API.

There are no analytics, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, bank connections, or CSV upload servers.

Delete or copy your data

Use “Export workspace” to download a copy. Use “Delete local data” twice to remove imported rows and signed reports.

You can also clear this site’s browser storage. Export first if you need a record.

Privacy requests

Email privacy@sociobot.in with a product privacy question.
